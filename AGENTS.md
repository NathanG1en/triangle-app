# Core Product Principles & Rules for Triangle Social Events

> **THE PURPOSE OF THIS APP IS TO MAKE IT AS EASY AS POSSIBLE FOR PEOPLE TO FIND OUT ABOUT EVENTS AND COORDINATE WITH EACH OTHER. IT'S SUPPOSED TO REDUCE FRICTION AND INCREASE VISIBILITY.**

## Principles
1. **Zero Friction Event Discovery**:
   - Every event must clearly state where it came from with a prominent, clickable direct link (`source_url`) to the original publication or ticketing page.
   - Users should never wonder "where is this coming from?" or "is this a real event?".

2. **Strict Event Quality**:
   - Filter out blog articles, general city guide posts (e.g., *"A Guide to Lenovo Center"*, *"If You Like Walking Tours"*), and SEO roundups that are NOT real time-bound social events or physical spots.
   - Do NOT assign arbitrary fake start times (e.g. `6:00 PM` or `7:34 AM`) to guide articles or non-events.
   - Physical events MUST have verified start times or be explicitly categorized as **Anytime Spots** (parks, trails, museums).

3. **Cohort Social Coordination**:
   - Make RSVPs (`Going`, `Interested`) and sharing effortless.
